FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Quick answers to what most customers ask before booking.

How long does a deep cleaning take?

A 1BHK usually takes 4–5 hours, a 2BHK 6–7 hours, and a 3BHK or larger home a full day. We'll give you an exact time when we confirm your booking.

Do I need to provide any cleaning materials?

No. Our team brings all equipment, machines and eco-friendly cleaning products. You only need to provide water and electricity.

Is it safe for children and pets?

Yes. We use safe, eco-friendly products. We just ask that children and pets stay away from the area while we are cleaning and until floors dry.

Do I need to be at home during the cleaning?

We recommend someone is present at the start and at the end for the final walk-through. During the cleaning you are free to carry on with your day.

What if I'm not happy with the result?

Tell us during the final walk-through and we'll fix it on the spot. If you notice something later, call us within 24 hours and we'll re-clean that area for free.

How do I pay, and are there any hidden charges?

The price we confirm before starting is the price you pay — no hidden charges. You can pay by cash, UPI or bank transfer after the work is done.
